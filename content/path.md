![](assets/logos/mark-swiss-army.png)

![](assets/logos/mark-cylg.svg)

### Under 16

#### Get your English ready, then join SPARC at 16

SPARC starts at 16, and everything in it is in English. Until then, that is the most important skill: you should understand technical language and follow YouTube videos, tech articles and blogs without translation tools.

- [Read about SPARC ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](/sparc/)

##### By when

Your 16th birthday

##### You need

- Technical English, without translation tools
- Curiosity for IT, no experience needed

##### Where

- Watch for onboarding dates: [events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch](https://events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch)

##### Optional head start

Want to do more? Look into networking basics. SPARC teaches all of this from zero, so this is only a recommendation. English comes first.

- IP addresses
- Subnetting
- DNS
- DHCP
- Routing
- TCP vs UDP
- VLANs
- Firewalls

### 16+

#### Sign up for a SPARC onboarding event

Register online for an event, then go in person. After onboarding, start with courses 1010 and 2010.

- [Read about SPARC ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](/sparc/)

##### By when

Until RS starts, at the latest by the end of your 24th year

##### You need

- Valid ID or passport (no driving licence)
- Your AHV number
- A laptop and a smartphone

##### Where

- Register for an event: [events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch](https://events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch)

### Before recruitment

#### Join SPARC and attend the orientation day

Not in SPARC yet? Register online for an onboarding event, go in person and start with courses 1010 and 2010. The orientation day is compulsory for men and voluntary for women. Recruitment follows and takes 2 days.

- [Read about SPARC ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](/sparc/)

##### By when

Until your recruitment

##### You need

- Valid ID or passport (no driving licence)
- Your AHV number
- A laptop and a smartphone
- For recruitment: the documents your cantonal military authority sends you

##### Where

- Register for a SPARC event: [events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch](https://events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch)
- Recruitment information: [armee.ch/de/rekrutierung](https://www.armee.ch/de/rekrutierung)

### Recruited

#### Use the time until RS for SPARC

If you make it your goal, there is easily enough time to do SPARC before RS. Not in SPARC yet? Register for an onboarding event. If your RS date collides with studies or work, you can file a deferral request: you ask the Army to move your RS to a later date. You need a valid reason, and approval is not guaranteed.

- [Read about SPARC ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](/sparc/)

##### By when

Before RS starts

Deferral request: up to about 4 months before RS starts

##### You need

- For SPARC: valid ID or passport and your AHV number
- For a deferral: a valid reason, such as a study conflict

##### Where

- Register for a SPARC event: [events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch](https://events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch)
- Deferral requests: [armee.ch/de/militaerdienst-dienstverschiebung](https://www.armee.ch/de/militaerdienst-dienstverschiebung)

### RS starts soon

#### Register for SPARC anyway

Every course helps, even if you cannot finish before RS. Start with the basics and keep going as far as you get.

- [Read about SPARC ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](/sparc/)

##### By when

As soon as possible

##### You need

- Valid ID or passport
- Your AHV number

##### Where

- Register for an event: [events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch](https://events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch)

### In RS

#### Tell your superior you want the CYLG selection

This works whatever function you were recruited for. The selection runs during basic training.

- [Read about CyLG ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](/cylg/)

##### By when

RS week 1

##### You need

- Fit for service
- Willing to do NCO training

##### Where

- No reply after week 1? Email: [cyber.lvbfu@vtg.admin.ch](mailto:cyber.lvbfu@vtg.admin.ch)

### After RS

#### Join Cyber Bataillon 42 through lateral entry

Register your interest, send a dossier, pass the selection and file a transfer request.

- [Read about Cy Bat 42 ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](/cybat42/)

##### By when

While you have more than 50 service days left

##### You need

- Fit for service
- A clean record

##### Where

- Requirements and interest form: [vtg.admin.ch/de/cyber-miliz](https://www.vtg.admin.ch/de/cyber-miliz)
