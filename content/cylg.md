# Cyber Lehrgang

![](assets/logos/mark-cylg.svg)

The Swiss Army’s course that turns recruits into militia cyber specialists. You do not apply before service: you ask for selection in your first week of recruit school.

- [How to get in ![](assets/icons/caret-down.svg)](#how-to-get-in)
- [Official Cyber-Miliz page ![](assets/icons/arrow-up-right.svg)](https://www.vtg.admin.ch/de/cyber-miliz)

> SPARC is not required and does not guarantee a place. The selection in recruit school decides.

## At a glance

- Duration: 40 weeks, incl. 6 weeks of basic training
- Location: Kaserne Jassbach (BE)
- Rank: Sergeant
- Class size: About 18 to 25
- Since: 2018

## What the CYLG is

A militia cyber course run by the Army. It is easy to mix up with two other things on this site.

Source: vtg.admin.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### SPARC

![](assets/icons/graduation-cap.svg)

Voluntary online training before recruit school, built by private companies. No military service involved.

### Normal recruit school

![](assets/icons/flag.svg)

18 weeks in the function you were recruited for. The CYLG is a separate, longer course.

### Cyber Bataillon 42

![](assets/icons/shield-check.svg)

A militia unit where many graduates are posted afterwards for their yearly refresher courses.

## Is it for you?

You need all the requirements below, plus one of the accepted backgrounds.

Source: vtg.admin.ch · armee.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Requirements

- Fit for military service
- A passed extended personnel security check (PSP)
- Willing to continue to NCO leadership training
- Resilient, quick to grasp things, discreet, modest and responsible

### Plus one of these backgrounds

- An ICT apprenticeship: Informatiker/in, Mediamatiker/in or ICT-Fachmann/-frau EFZ
- A science-focused Matura
- Studies in computer science, physics, mathematics, electrical engineering, automation or pedagogy
- Comprehensive self-taught knowledge (what counts as comprehensive is not published)

### Women

![](assets/icons/users-three.svg)

Every function is open. Service is voluntary for you, so register actively for recruitment.

### Already finished RS?

![](assets/icons/path.svg)

The CYLG is closed to you, but lateral entry into Cyber Bataillon 42 is still possible.

### Civilian service

![](assets/icons/prohibit.svg)

The CYLG is a military course, so choosing civilian service rules it out.

## What you learn

The official task areas, plus the last published split of the course hours.

Source: vtg.admin.ch · CYLG factsheet 86.055.01 · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Task areas

What the Army says graduates work on.

| Code | Task area | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DEV | Software tools | Develop software tools. |
| ANA | Cyber analysis | Analyse cyber events, attacks and vulnerabilities. |
| THR | Threat analysis | Assess threats to the Army’s ICT systems. |
| INC | Incident management | Manage cyber incidents. |
| FOR | Forensics | Run technical and forensic investigations. |
| SIT | Situation analysis | Analyse the cyber situation. |
| EDU | Training | Support and train troops. |

### Course hours in 2020

Historical factsheet, about 800 hours in total. The phases have changed since.

| Hours | Phase | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 60h | General foundations | Army, ethics and law. |
| 170h | Technical foundations | ICT, cryptology and applied security. |
| 30h | Cross-training | Listed without further detail. |
| 90h | Leadership | NCO leadership training. |
| 300h | Specialist training | Classified. Specialisations in 2020: Computer Network Operations, Cyber Fusion Center and Cyber Defence. |
| 150h | Deployment and exercises | Applying the skills in practice. |

## How to get in

There is no application before service. Everything happens in recruit school.

Source: vtg.admin.ch · nau.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

1. Prepare before RS
   Get comfortable with Linux, networking and Python. SPARC is the recommended way: since 2024 the CYLG builds on its content.
2. Speak up in week 1
   Tell your superior you want to take part in the selection, whatever function you were recruited for.
3. Follow up if nothing happens
   No reply after the first week? Email cyber.lvbfu@vtg.admin.ch.
4. Pass the selection
   Two stages plus the extended security check, during basic training (AGA).
5. Transfer to the CYLG
   Selected recruits leave their school after week 6 and continue in the CYLG.

### What selection looks like

![](assets/icons/eye.svg)

The two stages are not published. For the 2018 pilot, media reported an online test and a two-day assessment.

### If you are not selected

![](assets/icons/compass.svg)

You carry on in your normal RS function. Rules for trying again are not published.

## During the course

What daily life looks like, as far as it is public.

Source: vtg.admin.ch · ch.ch · Army function finder · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Location

![](assets/icons/map-trifold.svg)

Kaserne Jassbach (BE), about 1,000 m up. A cyber building with 50 workstations opened there in 2024.

### Language

![](assets/icons/translate.svg)

Training is in English. The 2020 factsheet also asked for German or French.

### Pay

![](assets/icons/medal.svg)

CHF 6 per day, tax-free, plus income compensation (EO) of at least CHF 69 per day. Cadre ranks earn more.

### Discretion

![](assets/icons/detective.svg)

Participants stay anonymous until the end of their deployment. The specialist training is classified.

## Commitments and deadlines

Plan these early, especially if you study or work.

Source: armee.ch · BFH · CYLG factsheet · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

- Leadership training
  NCO school is part of the course. The total service obligation for this rank is 440 days.
- RS deferral request
  About 4 months before RS, with a valid reason such as a study conflict. Hobbies alone do not count.
- Studies and work
  The course takes about 10 months.
- Underperforming
  The 2020 factsheet says you can be put on probation and moved to another function.

## What you get

Source: vtg.admin.ch · HSLU · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### You get

- Promotion to Sergeant
- Simplified admission to the federal Cyber Security Specialist exam
- A leadership certificate recognised in civilian life
- A completion certificate with an extended diploma supplement
- ECTS credits, for example 21 at HSLU. Each university decides.

### You do not get

- A guaranteed posting: graduates go to Cyber Bataillon 42 or Elektronische Abteilung 46, depending on Army needs
- A guaranteed civilian job
