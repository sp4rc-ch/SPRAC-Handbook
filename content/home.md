# Your path into Swiss Army **cyber.**

SPRAC Handbook is an unofficial, student-made knowledge base on SPARC, the Cyber Lehrgang and Cyber Bataillon 42. What each program is, how it works, who can join and where to read more.

- [Explore programs ![](assets/icons/arrow-right.svg)](#programs)
- [See the path](#)

## Three programs, one path.

Start with SPARC before recruit school, join the Cyber Lehrgang during RS and serve in Cyber Bataillon 42 after. Here is the short version of each.

> SPARC improves your chances; it is not a ticket in. Selection for the Cyber Lehrgang happens in recruit school.

### SPARC

![](assets/logos/mark-sparc-course.svg)

The Army's free, self-paced cyber pre-training. Explorer, Defender and Operator levels, all in English.

- Who: Swiss, 16+, before RS
- Cost: Free
- Time: 200-300 h online

[Read more](/sparc/)

### CyLG

![](assets/logos/mark-cylg.svg)

The 40-week Cyber Lehrgang at Jassbach barracks. Technical and leadership training, ending as sergeant.

- Apply: In RS week 1
- Length: 40 weeks · 800 h
- Result: sergeant, federal exam access

[Read more](/cylg/)

### Cy Bat 42

![](assets/logos/mark-cybat-42.svg)

Cyber Bataillon 42, the militia unit doing real cyber defence for Kommando Cyber since 2022.

- Since: 1 January 2022
- Service: About 3 weeks Refresher Course per year
- Entry: CyLG or lateral entry

[Read more](#)
