# Cyber Bataillon 42

![](assets/logos/mark-cybat-42.svg)

The militia cyber battalion that backs the Army’s professional cyber command. You serve here after the Cyber Lehrgang, or join later through lateral entry.

- [Check lateral entry ![](assets/icons/caret-down.svg)](#join-through-lateral-entry)
- [Official Cyber-Miliz page ![](assets/icons/arrow-up-right.svg)](https://www.vtg.admin.ch/de/cyber-miliz)

> No official source guarantees a posting here. Placement follows the Army’s needs and your skills.

## At a glance

- Type: Militia battalion
- Location: Bern
- WK: About 3 weeks a year
- Total service: 440 days as Sergeant
- Short name: Cy Bat 42

## What it does

It supports Kommando Cyber in all situations. In plain terms: it protects the Army’s own information systems and networks, and keeps enough trained people available over time.

Source: vtg.admin.ch · Army flyer · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Cyber defence

![](assets/icons/shield-check.svg)

Detecting and defending against foreign cyber actors.

### Situation picture

![](assets/icons/eye.svg)

Maintaining the military cyber situation picture.

### Cryptology

![](assets/icons/key.svg)

Named as a core task next to defence and the situation picture.

## Join through lateral entry

Already finished recruit school? You can still join if you have service days left.

Source: vtg.admin.ch · cybat42.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

1. Register your interest
   Use the “Cyber in der Armee” form on the Cyber-Miliz page.
2. Submit a dossier
   It is the basis for the selection.
3. Pass the selection
   Two stages, run as an assessment centre according to the old cybat42.ch.
4. File a transfer request
   The Umteilungsgesuch, signed by your current unit commander.

### Who can apply

![](assets/icons/check-circle.svg)

Fit for service, interested in cyber, a clean record and more than 50 service days left.

### Coming from the CYLG?

![](assets/icons/graduation-cap.svg)

You do not need this route. Graduates are placed directly, here or in Elektronische Abteilung 46.

## Service

What membership means for your calendar.

Source: armee.ch · BFH · cybat42.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

- Refresher courses
  Served in detachments throughout the year rather than in one block.
- Deferral request
  At least 14 weeks before the WK, with a valid reason. There is no right to approval.
- Call-up dates
  Published on armee.ch every year, from 1 July.
- Pay and compensation
  Income compensation (EO) applies. Pay depends on your rank.
- Between service periods
  What preparation is expected between refresher courses is not published.

## Growth and limits

Source: vtg.admin.ch · VBS · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Where you can grow

- Cadre training and specialist courses in Switzerland and abroad
- Certifications
- International exercises such as NATO’s Locked Shields, where a Swiss team placed second in 2026
- A military cyber career, up to the officer path

### What you cannot share

- Details of the classified specialist training
- Anything the Army has not published itself
- When unsure, ask Kommando Cyber before you post
