# SPARC

![](assets/logos/mark-sparc-course.svg)

The Swiss Army’s cyber training programme for young people, from IT basics up to a cyber security role you choose.

- [Find an onboarding event ![](assets/icons/arrow-up-right.svg)](https://events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch)
- [Official SPARC site ![](assets/icons/arrow-up-right.svg)](https://sparc-cyberdefence.ch)

> SPARC improves your chances, it is not a ticket in. Selection for the Cyber Lehrgang happens in recruit school.

## At a glance

- Cost: Free, you pay travel to experience days
- Time: 200 to 300 h
- Age: 16 or older
- Language: English
- Format: Online + experience days

## What SPARC is

Private Swiss companies build and run SPARC, with Consulteer AG as operator, and the Army pays for it. SPARC is not part of the Army itself, and the name is not an acronym: it comes from “sparkling”.

Source: sparc-cyberdefence.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

## Is it for you?

You need no IT background: the Explorer level starts from zero. What matters most is good English and the discipline to learn on your own.

Source: sparc-cyberdefence.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Requirements

- Swiss citizenship
- A laptop or PC and a smartphone with a current Android or iOS

### Swiss abroad

![](assets/icons/globe.svg)

Possible. Onboarding has to be in person and the registered letter needs a Swiss address.

### Women

![](assets/icons/users-three.svg)

Nothing in the SPARC rules excludes you, and SPARC links to Women in Cyber.

### RS in a few weeks?

![](assets/icons/clock.svg)

Still worth it. SPARC says every course adds value, even if you cannot finish.

## What you learn

Three levels that build on each other.

Source: sparc-cyberdefence.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Explorer

IT basics you need for everything after.

| Code | Course | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1010 | Discover the Cyber World | Use the platform, know the SPARC and military cyber path, recognise common threats and protect yourself. |
| 2010 | Basic Skills of a Cyber Warrior | Problem solving, learning technique, critical thinking, ethics and SMART goals. |
| 2020 | Coding Basics in Python | Variables, conditions, loops, functions, recursion, classes and inheritance. |
| 2040 | Network Engineering Basics | OSI and TCP/IP, Wireshark, switching, routing, firewalls, DNS, DHCP, SSH and encryption. |
| 2050 | Linux Engineering Basics | Command line, users and permissions, packages, network setup, processes and services. |
| 2060 | Windows Engineering Basics | Windows client and server, Active Directory, group policies, LDAP, Kerberos and PowerShell. |
| 2070 | Web Technologies Basics | HTML, CSS, JavaScript, HTTP and HTTPS, REST APIs, SQL, backend and cookie authentication. |

### Defender

The core concepts of cyber security and a defender’s skills.

| Code | Course | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3010 | Security Basics, Frameworks, Blue Team | Core security concepts and defender skills. Courses 3010, 3011 and 3020. |
| 3080 | Open Source Intelligence and Operations | Finding and using open source intelligence. |
| 3140 | Network, Linux, Windows or Web Security | Specialise in one technology area. Courses 3140 to 3170. |

### Operator

Both levels combined, in depth, in one role you choose.

| Code | Course | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4010 | Cyber Defence | Blue team: protect and defend systems. |
| 4020 | Offensive Operations | Red team: attack to find weaknesses. |
| 4030 | Digital Forensics and Incident Response | Investigate incidents and find out what happened. |
| 4040 | Cyber Threat Intelligence | Track and analyse threat actors. |

## How it works

A mix of online learning and in-person days.

Source: sparc-cyberdefence.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### Self-paced

![](assets/icons/clock.svg)

No classes and no set learning times. Spread over six months, 250 hours is roughly 10 hours a week.

### Labs and quizzes

![](assets/icons/terminal-window.svg)

Hands-on challenges run in virtual labs and are checked automatically. Quizzes and tests are for self-checking.

### Support on Threema

![](assets/icons/users-three.svg)

Ask the SPARC team on Threema. A big community of SPARC students helps with problems there too.

### Experience days

![](assets/icons/calendar-blank.svg)

The only fixed dates: up to 75 people, workshops, team challenges and expert talks.

## How to join

There is no online-only registration. Joining starts at an in-person onboarding event.

Source: sparc-cyberdefence.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

1. Sign up for an onboarding event
   Register in advance on events.sparc-cyberdefence.ch.
2. Go in person
   Bring a valid ID or passport, your AHV number and the completed registration form.
3. Activate your V-Login
   Register the V-Login as soon as the letter arrives.
4. Log in and start
   Use the yellow Login button on the SPARC site and take courses 1010 and 2010 first.
5. Set up your account
   Pick your hacker name under My Account and activate Threema.

### V-Login

![](assets/icons/key.svg)

Your login for the SPARC platform.

### Threema

![](assets/icons/lock.svg)

Used for event info and check-in. Keep your Threema Safe password, without it you need a new account.

## Rules and deadlines

SPARC is very flexible. These are the only limits.

Source: sparc-cyberdefence.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

- Finish before recruit school starts
  Or by the end of your 24th year.
- Attend at least one experience day
  It has a fixed date, so plan it in.
- Accounts inactive for two years are deleted
  Pausing just means not logging in. You can come back while you are still eligible.

## What you get

Source: sparc-cyberdefence.ch · vtg.admin.ch · Last verified 31 Oct 2026

### You get

- A certificate for each completed course
- The basics the Cyber Lehrgang builds on: since 2024 its content is based on SPARC

### You do not get

- An exam or an overall SPARC diploma, yet
- Formal recognition of SPARC itself
