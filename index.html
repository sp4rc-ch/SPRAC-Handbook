Spotted a problem? Open an issue.

Before launch we only take issues. Found a wrong fact, a broken link or a typo? Open an issue in the repo. Pull requests for new pages or UI changes are paused until launch. Contributions are only accepted from SPARC students and people associated with SPARC.

$ git checkout -b fix/typo

  • Wrong or outdated fact

    Dates, numbers, requirements or contacts that changed.

  • Broken link

    A link that 404s or points to the wrong page.

  • Typo or unclear text

    Spelling, grammar or confusing wording.

Privacy first: don't open issues or comment with a GitHub account that shows your real name or other personal info. Use a separate account, for example with your SPARC alias.
